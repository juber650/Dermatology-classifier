Dermatology Disease Classifier

Enter the clinical and histopathological findings for a patient. The model predicts one of six erythemato-squamous diseases.

Score each finding from 0 (absent) to 3 (most severe).

Histopathological findings

Score each finding from 0 (absent) to 3 (most severe).
